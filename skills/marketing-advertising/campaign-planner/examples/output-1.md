# Expected campaign plan

**Objective:** assess whether this two-week pilot obtains 12 qualified workshop registrations under the supplied qualification and logistics-consent definition. This is a decision threshold, not a forecast.

**Audience/message hypothesis:** independent bookkeepers may value a concrete workflow demonstration. Describe what the workshop actually covers; do not claim accreditation, guaranteed clients or income improvement.

| Budget allocation             | USD |
| ----------------------------- | --: |
| Landing page/copy preparation | 180 |
| Paid social pilot             | 240 |
| Two creative variants         | 120 |
| Contingency                   |  60 |
| Total ceiling                 | 600 |

These are allocations, not verified vendor prices. The existing newsletter is assumed to have no incremental send cost; confirm that before approval. Do not silently spend contingency or exclude creative cost from total reporting.

Use the newsletter as one owned-channel invitation within existing consent, and the single paid placement as a small pilot. Keep audience and offer stable while comparing the two message variants; do not declare a statistically significant winner from a handful of registrations.

Measurement: deduplicated qualified registrations, approved aggregate spend and the two-week window. At the threshold, total-budget cost per qualified registration is 600 / 12 = USD 50; paid-media-only reporting must be labeled separately. With zero registrations, cost per registration is undefined.

Before launch: approve claims, consent wording, landing page and tracking. Review spend and data quality during the pilot; pause if qualification cannot be measured or the budget cap is at risk. After two weeks, compare actual outcomes with the threshold and decide continue/revise/stop. No ads, emails, tracking or purchases were executed.
