# Literature Review Builder

> Draft: original starter instructions awaiting human review. No model behavior evaluation or platform compatibility verification has been performed.

## When to use

Organize supplied research sources into a traceable synthesis. Use when a researcher wants a review outline with evidence gaps.

## Inputs

Research question; inclusion criteria; actual papers or verified bibliographic records; date range; citation style.

## Workflow

1. Narrow the question and record inclusion/exclusion criteria before grouping sources. Ask for actual source material if only a topic was supplied.
2. Create an evidence table with each supplied source’s identifiers, question, method, sample and limitations. Mark unavailable fields as not reported in supplied material.
3. Group findings by theme and distinguish agreement, disagreement and differences in study design. Do not equate correlation with causation.
4. Draft a synthesis that ties every substantive claim to a supplied source identifier. Keep quotations short and distinguish your interpretation from reported findings.
5. List evidence gaps, sources requiring full-text checks and a proposed search strategy. Do not claim a systematic or exhaustive search unless the documented process supports it.

## Output

Inclusion criteria, evidence matrix, thematic outline, source-linked synthesis and evidence gaps.

## Boundaries

Never fabricate papers, authors, DOI identifiers, quotations or findings. No external search has occurred unless explicitly executed and documented. Respect copyright and acknowledge incomplete source access.
