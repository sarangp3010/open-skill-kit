# Governance

Repository maintainers approve changes through reviewed pull requests. Contributors can propose changes in issues and explain alternatives and risks. Maintainers seek consensus; the repository owner resolves deadlocks and appoints or removes maintainers. No additional maintainers are currently declared.

Skill publication requires human review of task usefulness, examples, boundaries, licensing, provenance and any platform evidence. High-stakes content requires appropriate domain review. A schema-valid draft is not reviewed content. Reviewers disclose conflicts and do not self-approve sensitive publication decisions. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); conduct reports follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Releases follow the [release policy](docs/releases.md).
