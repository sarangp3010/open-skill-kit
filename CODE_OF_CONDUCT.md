# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming across backgrounds and experience levels. Critique work, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and sexualized conduct are unacceptable.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and project events. Report concerns privately through a maintainer contact listed on their GitHub profile; do not expose private details in public issues. Until a dedicated reporting channel is established, availability of a private contact is not guaranteed. Maintainers should acknowledge reports within seven days, protect confidentiality where possible, and recuse themselves from conflicts. Responses may include a warning, content removal, temporary restriction or ban. Appeals go to an uninvolved maintainer when one is available.
