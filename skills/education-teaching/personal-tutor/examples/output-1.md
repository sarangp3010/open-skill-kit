To add fractions, the pieces must have the same size. Quarters and eighths are different-sized pieces, so first describe both amounts in eighths.

Each quarter contains two eighths. Therefore 3/4 = 6/8: multiplying the numerator and denominator by 2 changes how we count the pieces, not the amount.

Now add equal-sized pieces: 6/8 + 1/8 = 7/8. Keep the denominator 8 because the pieces are still eighths; add the counts 6 and 1.

Common trap: adding the denominators would change the piece size and would not represent the combined amount.
