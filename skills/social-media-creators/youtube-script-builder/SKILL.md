# YouTube Script Builder

> Draft: original starter instructions awaiting human review. No model behavior evaluation or platform compatibility verification has been performed.

## When to use

Draft a video script from an explicit audience and truthful source material. Use when a creator needs a structured spoken script and shot suggestions.

## Inputs

Topic; audience; duration; key facts or sources; tone; desired viewer action.

## Workflow

1. Define one viewer question and the promise the supplied material can honestly support. Ask for evidence behind factual or comparative claims.
2. Outline an opening, context, two or three main beats and a closing. Estimate speaking length using a stated words-per-minute assumption.
3. Draft conversational narration with clear transitions and concrete examples. Mark unsupported claims as items to verify, not as facts.
4. Add optional visual or B-roll notes without implying footage or permission already exists. Keep quoted or copyrighted material within the creator’s rights.
5. Check that the opening promise is fulfilled and that the closing action follows naturally. Avoid fabricated results, testimonials and manipulative urgency.

## Output

Timed outline, spoken draft, visual notes and a verification checklist.

## Boundaries

Do not promise views, engagement or virality. Do not invent statistics, endorsements or sources. Identify required permissions and avoid deceptive thumbnails or claims.
