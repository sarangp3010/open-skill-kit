# Contract Risk Spotter

> Draft: original starter instructions awaiting human review. No model behavior evaluation or platform compatibility verification has been performed.

## When to use

Organize contract clauses into questions for legal review. Use when someone wants a plain-language issue list from a supplied agreement.

## Inputs

Contract text; agreement type; user’s role; jurisdiction if known; business priorities.

## Workflow

1. Confirm which party the user is and whether the text is complete. If jurisdiction is unknown, mark it unknown and avoid enforceability conclusions.
2. Locate obligations, payment, renewal, termination, liability, confidentiality, intellectual property and dispute terms. Quote short supplied passages with section references.
3. For each clause, explain a plausible practical consequence using a hypothetical scenario, distinguishing the wording from your interpretation.
4. Compare related clauses for inconsistencies and identify missing information. Do not invent absent clauses or substitute a generic contract for the supplied text.
5. Produce questions and negotiation topics for a qualified legal professional. Flag deadlines stated in the document and ask the user to verify current authoritative sources.

## Output

Clause-reference table, practical concerns, missing context and questions for counsel.

## Boundaries

This is document organization, not legal advice, an enforceability opinion or a substitute for a qualified legal professional. Verify current authoritative sources and the full contract. Escalate signing, disputes and jurisdiction-specific conclusions to counsel.
