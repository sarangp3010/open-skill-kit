# Code of conduct

Open Skill Kit welcomes contributors across professions, skill levels and backgrounds. Be respectful, constructive and considerate. Discuss evidence and design tradeoffs; critique work rather than a person's identity or worth. Credit others' work and respect stated boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of private information and sustained personal attacks are unacceptable. This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, project discussions and project events, including external conduct when someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and privacy

Contact the repository owner privately through a contact method they publish on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/sarangp3010). Do not post private identities, screenshots, sensitive allegations or vulnerability details in an ordinary public issue. If no private method is listed, request a private reporting route without disclosing incident details. A dedicated confidential conduct channel is not yet configured or verified; see [readiness](docs/community-readiness.md). Security vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Provide only the information needed to understand the incident and your preferred contact method. Maintainers should protect confidentiality as far as practical, explain any necessary sharing, and avoid promises of absolute secrecy. Do not retaliate against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement and appeals

Maintainers assess the reported behavior and context, communicate the applicable expectation, and may request a correction, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, remove content or ban a participant. Serious or repeated harm can warrant immediate restriction. Maintainers must disclose conflicts and recuse themselves when involved in an incident.

Acknowledgement within seven days is a target, not a guaranteed response time. An affected participant may request reconsideration with relevant evidence through the same private route. An uninvolved maintainer should review appeals when available; currently only the repository owner is declared, so independent escalation cannot be guaranteed. No additional enforcement authority or reporting service is implied. This is an original project policy, not a claim of certification by another organization.
