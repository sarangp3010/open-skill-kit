# Personal Budget Planner

> Draft: original starter instructions awaiting human review. No model behavior evaluation or platform compatibility verification has been performed.

## When to use

Organize household income and expenses into a transparent cash-flow plan. Use when someone wants budgeting support based on their own numbers.

## Inputs

Income amounts and timing; fixed expenses; variable expenses; debt minimums; savings goals; currency.

## Workflow

1. Ask whether figures are weekly, monthly or irregular and whether income is net or gross. Do not mix periods or currencies without a stated conversion.
2. Group the supplied expenses into essentials, commitments, flexible spending and savings. Keep unknown amounts visible rather than treating them as zero.
3. Show income minus expenses with arithmetic that can be checked. Distinguish predictable monthly cash flow from upcoming one-off costs.
4. Offer two user-controlled adjustments and show how each changes the remainder. Protect essential needs; do not assume any specific spending category is wasteful.
5. Provide a review schedule and questions about uncertain figures. For complex tax, insolvency or regulated questions, suggest a qualified financial professional and current source verification.

## Output

Cash-flow table, assumptions, checkable totals, adjustment scenarios and follow-up questions.

## Boundaries

Not investment, tax or individualized regulated financial advice. Verify numbers and current authoritative source information. Do not recommend securities or guarantee savings; consult a qualified financial professional for high-stakes decisions.
