# Construction Change Order Organizer

> Draft: original instructions with fictional examples. Independent domain review and real agent execution are pending.

## When to use

Turn supplied scope-change notes into a traceable cost and schedule review packet; use when a project coordinator prepares a change request for authorized review. Keep the task limited to the supplied records and the requester’s intended decision.

## Inputs

Baseline scope/version; change request ID; stated quantities, rates and currency; schedule assumptions; approvals matrix; exclusions and supporting records.

## Workflow

1. Anchor the requested change to the supplied baseline drawing/specification version and request ID. Separate changed scope from original-scope corrections and unconfirmed field conditions.

2. Itemize quantities, units, labor/material rates and stated markups. Show each extension and keep allowances, estimates and documented quotations distinct.

3. Calculate the incremental subtotal, then separately list tax, contingency or credits only where supplied. Do not treat a price estimate as an approved contract change.

4. Map the claimed schedule effect to named activities and prerequisites. A five-day task is not automatically a five-day completion delay; ask the scheduler to confirm dependencies and float.

5. List design, safety, permit and access questions for responsible professionals. Record missing evidence without approving methods, interpreting codes or authorizing site work.

6. Return a draft review packet with requested decision, source attachments and sign-off roles. Keep approval pending until the authorized reviewer responds; do not instruct crews or notify contractual parties automatically.

## Output

- Change summary and baseline references.
- itemized cost table.
- stated exclusions.
- schedule dependency questions.
- design/safety review blockers.
- approval matrix.
- decision requested.

## Boundaries

Not an estimate certification, engineering design, legal notice, permit check or safety approval. Do not supply hazardous work methods or infer authority to start work. Qualified project professionals must verify site conditions and applicable requirements.
