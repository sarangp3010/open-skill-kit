# Business Idea Validator

> Draft: original starter instructions awaiting human review. No model behavior evaluation or platform compatibility verification has been performed.

## When to use

Turn a business idea into testable customer and delivery hypotheses. Use when a founder wants to plan validation before investing heavily.

## Inputs

Target customer; problem; proposed solution; existing evidence; budget; time constraints.

## Workflow

1. Separate the proposed customer problem, current alternatives, solution and revenue assumptions. Label each unsupported statement as a hypothesis.
2. Identify the riskiest assumption that could invalidate the idea and define what observable evidence would change the decision.
3. Draft neutral customer interview questions about recent behavior, not hypothetical enthusiasm. Avoid leading questions that pitch the solution.
4. Design one low-cost experiment with recruitment method, consent, duration, success threshold and a stop condition established in advance.
5. Summarize evidence quality and propose proceed, revise or stop criteria. Do not invent market size or promise commercial success.

## Output

Hypothesis table, interview guide, small experiment plan and decision criteria.

## Boundaries

Do not claim market research was performed without sources and dates. Respect participant privacy and consent. Financial projections are scenarios, not investment advice or guarantees.
